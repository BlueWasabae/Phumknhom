# ភូមិខ្ញុំ (Phum Khnhom) — Open World, Village Life-Sim & Side-Scrolling Boss Cavern Expansion

## 1. The Open World: 8 Interconnected Biomes & 3 World Acts

Instead of a single town menu, players roam a seamless top-down open world on foot, by mount (Tuk-Tuk, Water Buffalo, Hover-Lotus), or via Fast-Travel Shrines. Every biome has its own forageables, fish, secret shrines, NPCs, and **Side-Scrolling Dungeon/Boss Entrances**.

| Open-World Region | Atmosphere & Life-Sim Resources | Side-Scrolling Sub-Zones & Bosses |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Phum & Central Plaza** *(Safe Hub)* | Personal Farm, Artisan Sheds, Shops, Snack Carts, Pagoda Bundles, Lucky Wheel, Multi-Channel Chat (`AREA 0–9`) | Underground Root Cellar (Starter Tutorial Cave) |
| **2. Emerald Rice Valley** | Muddy ponds, wild Trakuon, frogs, bamboo groves, riverside fishing piers | *Muddy Rice Hollow*, *Bamboo Wind Grotto* |
| **3. Sunken Temple Ruins** | Ancient mossy sandstone pillars, buried relics (hoe-dig spots), rare lotus ponds | *Ancient Stone Crypt*, *Sunken Lotus Vault* |
| **4. Red Laterite Highlands & Mines** | Multi-floor deep mining shaft (Copper → Iron → Gold → Mythril → Astral Ore), crystal nodes | *Obsidian Magma Core*, *Clockwork Forge* |
| **5. Whispering Palm Jungle** | Exotic mangoes, durians, rare butterflies/insects, hidden herbalist hut | *Burning Palm Canopy*, *Venom Hive* |
| **6. Sapphire Coast & Coral Pier** | Deep-sea ocean fishing, Night Market boat, crab traps, sunken treasure diving | *Abyssal Trench* (Deep-sea dark pressure cavern) |
| **7. Blighted Marsh** *(Awakens in Act II)* | Corrupted violet flora, cursed mushrooms, high-risk rare foraging | *Plague Nest*, *Shadow Rift* |
| **8. Sky Pagoda & Astral Spire** *(Act II–III)* | Floating island platforms reached via high-tier Wings or Sky Shrine | *Cloud Citadel*, *The Eclipse Sanctum*, *Void Horizon* |

### World Progression States (3-Act Phum Khnhom Progression)
* **Act I — Village Awakening (Pre-Hardmode):** Players farm, restore the Village Pagoda, mine starter ores, and clear Act I bosses.
* **Act II — The Shattered Seal (Hardmode):** Defeating the Act I Gatekeeper (*Sovereign Gatekeeper*) permanently unlocks **Hardmode** for your character/party—new high-tier ores spawn in the mines, the *Blighted Marsh* opens, tougher enemies drop rare crafting souls, and Act II bosses awaken.
* **Act III — Mythic Trials of the Ancient Gods (Post-Endgame):** After defeating *Rahu (Devourer of the Moon)*, the world enters **Mythic Tier**: cosmic bullet-hell superbosses, God-tier weapon trees, and the **Boss Rush Arena**.

## 2. Side-Scrolling Boss Cavern Combat Engine

### 5 Distinct Playable Classes
Players aren't locked to one class—your equipped **Armor Set + Weapon + Accessories** defines your build (swappable anytime via Outfit Presets):
1. **Melee (Warrior):** Broadswords, spears, flails, boomerangs, high defense, shockwave projectiles on endgame blades.
2. **Ranged (Marksman):** Bamboo bows, crossbows, elemental blowguns, handcannons, specialized ammo (Chili Fire Arrows, Lotus Piercing Bolts).
3. **Magic (Mystic):** Staves, ancient palm-leaf spell tomes, summonable laser prisms; powered by the Energy/MP bar and Lotus Teas.
4. **Summoner (Spirit Tamer):** Whips + combat spirit minions (Floating Mini-Nagas, Paper Lantern Wisps, Stone Golems) that fight automatically while you dodge.
5. **Rogue / Shadow (Khmer Stealth):** Throwing knives, chakrams, bombs, and a **Stealth Bar** that fills when not attacking to unleash massive *Stealth Strikes*.

### Mythic Boss Combat Mechanics & Loadouts
* **Focus Gauge *(Adrenaline Equivalent)*:** Fills gradually during a boss fight as long as you take **0 damage**. Reaching 100% lets you trigger a 5-second hyper-burst (**+120% Damage**). Taking a hit resets the gauge—rewarding clean bullet-hell dodging.
* **Fury Gauge *(Rage Equivalent)*:** Fills when taking hits or standing dangerously close to the boss. Activating it grants **+40% Attack Speed & +25% Crit** for 7 seconds.
* **Accessory & Mobility Loadout (6 Slots + 1 Wing Slot):** Equip Dash Boots, Double-Jump Clouds, Grappling Hooks, Shield of Knockback Immunity, Life-Regen Charms, and Class Emblems.

## 3. Deep Village Homestead Life-Sim Systems

### 1. Four-Season Calendar & Weather Cycle
Each season lasts **14 in-game days** (synced server-wide so everyone celebrates festivals together), changing visuals, background music, forageables, fish, and available crops:
* **Season 1 — Dry Bloom (Spring):** Bamboo shoots, Trakuon, Jasmine Rice, Pink Lotus. *Festival:* **Khmer New Year Water Splash & Kite Contest**.
* **Season 2 — Monsoon Rains (Summer):** Frequent auto-watering storms, Chili, Kampot Pepper, Tropical Durian, lightning rods charge **Battery Packs** for tech crafting. *Festival:* **Riverside Boat Race**.
* **Season 3 — Golden Harvest (Autumn):** Sweet Pumpkin, Golden Saffron, Giant Yam, rare mushrooms in the jungle. *Festival:* **Moon Lantern Night & Harvest Fair** (show off Gold-Star crops in a 9-item display box for prizes).
* **Season 4 — Cool Mist (Winter):** Outdoor soil rests (only **Frost Lotus** grows outside, unless you unlock the **Greenhouse**), heavy focus on Ice-Cave Mining, Deep-Sea Fishing, Artisan aging, and Boss Raiding. *Festival:* **Night Market Pier** (3-day traveling boat bazaar selling rare exotic seeds, furniture, and mystery relics).

### 2. Artisan Processing Machines (Multiply Crop Profits 2x–5x)
In Phum Khnhom, raw crops are only step one—building machines on your farm turns them into high-value goods and **Mythic-tier Raid Potions**:
* **Rice Mill & Flour Grinder:** Turns Jasmine Rice → Fine Flour / Rice Noodles.
* **Palm Sugar Boiler:** Turns Sugar Palm sap → Caramelized Palm Sugar (energy boost base).
* **Fermentation Clay Jar:** Turns Vegetables/Fish → Artisan Pickles & **Royal Prahok** (high Coin value + Defense food).
* **Lotus Tea & Elixir Brewer (Keg):** Turns Pink Lotus, Mango, or Golden Durian → **Aged Fruit Wine & Spirit Elixirs** (massive Mythic Raid stat buffs).
* **Silk Loom:** Turns Golden Silk Moth threads → **Royal Hol/Pamuong Silk Cloth** (required to craft endgame Magic/Rogue robes & luxury Plaza outfits).
* **Cellar Aging Cask:** Placed inside your upgraded house cellar; ages Elixirs & Cheeses over real days from Normal → Silver → Gold → **Iridium/Mythic Quality** (doubles buff duration & Coin value).
* **Smoker & Dehydrator:** Preserves caught rare fish and fruits for portable dungeon healing stacks.

### 3. The Ancient Pagoda Restoration (Community Center Bundles)
At the edge of the village stands a ruined **Ancient Pagoda**. Completing 6 themed Offering Altars restores the Pagoda and unlocks massive permanent features:
1. **Harvest Altar** *(Seasonal Crops & Artisan Goods)* → **Unlocks the Glass Greenhouse** on your farm (grow any season's crops & Golden Durian year-round!).
2. **River Altar** *(Seasonal & Biome Fish)* → **Repairs the Broken Ferry Boat** to Sapphire Coast & Abyssal Trench.
3. **Forage Altar** *(Wild herbs, shells, jungle fruit)* → **Repairs the Jungle Suspension Bridge** to Whispering Palm Jungle.
4. **Blacksmith Altar** *(Ores, Bars & Boss Cores)* → **Repairs the Mine Cart & Fast-Travel Shrines** across all 8 biomes.
5. **Kitchen Altar** *(Cooked Khmer dishes & snacks)* → **Unlocks the Master Chef Cart** (20% cheaper buffs + automated cooking queue).
6. **Spirit Altar** *(Rare Dungeon Relics & Boss Shards)* → **Unlocks the Shrine of Blessings** (grants a daily random +10% Luck, Drop Rate, or Farm Yield buff).

### 4. NPC Villagers, 10-Heart Friendship & Marriage/Roommates
Populate the village with memorable, scheduled NPCs who walk between their homes, shops, and the Plaza depending on time of day:
* **10-Heart System:** Chat daily, fulfill bulletin-board Help Wanted requests, and gift their Loved items (each NPC has unique Loves/Hates).
* **Heart Cutscenes & Perks:** Hitting 3, 6, and 8 Hearts triggers short visual-novel story events and mails you **exclusive cooking recipes, weapon blueprints, or shop discounts**.
* **Spouse / Farm Roommate (10 Hearts):** Present a *Lotus Pendant* to invite your favorite NPC to move into your upgraded farmhouse. They help water crops, feed livestock, repair fences, and hand you a freshly cooked **Dungeon Buff Snack** each morning.
* **Multiplayer Co-op Marriage:** Two real players who reach max `Friendly` level can also exchange a *Golden Ring* to share a **Co-op Farm & Joint House**!

### 5. Museum, Artifacts, Foraging & House Decorating
* **Village Museum & Bestiary:** Hoe up wiggly dirt spots in the open world, crack geodes from the mines, catch legendary fish, and defeat bosses to fill the Museum. Hitting milestone donations (10, 25, 50, 100 items) awards **permanent Max HP upgrades, extra Accessory Slots, Rare Furniture, and the Ancient Seed**.
* **Farmhouse Tiers & Boss Trophies:**
  * *Tier 1:* Cozy Khmer Bamboo Stilt Hut.
  * *Tier 2:* Adds Kitchen (Cooking) + Bedroom.
  * *Tier 3:* Adds Upstairs Loft + **Boss Trophy Wall** (hang glowing trophies from bosses for passive farm-wide aura buffs!).
  * *Tier 4:* Adds **Underground Aging Cellar** (for Mythic Casks) + Personal Teleport Pad straight to your chosen Dungeon Biome.
